# career_context.md

This file describes the PM's professional identity, career trajectory, and domain expertise. It is permanent and travels across every role. It tells the AI what level of PM it is working with, what assumptions are safe to make, and what framing to use.

## Level and scope

- Operating at L7-L8 (Staff / Principal PM equivalent)
- Owns product strategy across multiple surfaces or a major product line
- Regularly presents to VP and C-suite stakeholders
- Expected to drive alignment, not just document it

## Domain expertise

- AI-native product development — deep fluency with LLM products, agentic systems, and AI tooling
- FAANG-scale execution: large cross-functional teams, multi-quarter roadmaps, high-stakes launches
- Early-stage AI startups: 0-to-1, rapid iteration, resource-constrained prioritization

## Working style

- Prefers to see the tension behind a recommendation, not just the recommendation
- Asks for the two or three options that were considered before the chosen path
- Does not want summaries of what was already said — wants net-new synthesis
- Treats AI as a thinking partner, not a drafting tool

## How to use this file

When generating any output, calibrate depth and framing to this profile. Do not explain basic PM concepts. Do not over-hedge. Write as if addressing a peer who shares this background and will push back on anything that isn't sharp.
