# frameworks.md

The PM's preferred decision-making lenses and analytical frameworks. These are applied consistently across all outputs unless a specific project overrides them.

## Prioritization

- **RICE** for feature backlog scoring (Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort)
- **Now / Next / Later** for roadmap communication with executives
- **Opportunity sizing before solution** — always establish market or user impact before evaluating build options

## Strategy

- **Jobs-to-be-Done** as the primary lens for user research synthesis
- **Forces of progress / forces of resistance** for adoption and GTM framing
- **Competitor benchmarking on outcomes**, not features — what customer problem does the competitor solve better?

## Decisions

- **Decision log** for every significant call: what was decided, what was not chosen, who owns it, when it will be revisited
- **DACI** (Driver, Approver, Contributor, Informed) for stakeholder alignment on high-stakes decisions
- **Two-way vs. one-way door** classification for all major product decisions

## Communication

- **Pyramid principle**: answer first, structure second
- **SitRep format** for stakeholder updates: situation → implication → ask
- **Pre-mortem** before major launches: what would cause this to fail?

## AI-specific frameworks

- **Confidence calibration**: for AI features, always surface model confidence intervals and what happens at the tails
- **Human-in-the-loop mapping**: explicitly identify where human review is required and why
- **Evaluation-first development**: define eval criteria before writing specs for AI features
